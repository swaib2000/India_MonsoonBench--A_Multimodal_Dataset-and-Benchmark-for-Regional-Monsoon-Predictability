Forecasting Formulation

Autoregressive monthly rainfall anomaly forecasting.

The benchmark is designed to study monsoon transitions using regional sliding-window forecasting. For each spatial patch, the model observes three consecutive months of multimodal predictors and predicts the next month rainfall anomaly class.

Mathematical Formulation

Patch-wise monthly AR task

Given X(s, t-2), X(s, t-1), X(s, t), predict Y(s, t+1)

Here s is a spatial patch from a state, t is a calendar month, X is the multimodal predictor stack, and Y is the rainfall anomaly class. Equivalently, for target month m, the input months are m-3, m-2, m-1 and the target horizon is 1 month.

The task is trained separately per climatic region to avoid mixing distinct rainfall-generating mechanisms and to expose region-specific predictability.

Regional Benchmark Design

Four climatic regions used for per-region training

Each region is trained independently. This formulation is more physically meaningful than a single pooled national model because monsoon mechanisms differ across orographic, coastal, arid, and central monsoon regimes.

Target Definition

Rainfall class from IMD long-period average

Ground truth labels are ordinal rainfall anomaly categories. For each month, observed rainfall is divided by the state's monthly long-period average rainfall normal. The same logic is applied to annual totals by using the sum of monthly normals.

ratio(s, m) = observed rainfall(s, m) / IMD monthly LPA(s, m)

IMD Normals

Long-period average rainfall values used for ground truth

Values are monthly rainfall normals in millimeters. They are used only for label generation, not as model input.

Monsoon Transitions

Transition-specific evaluation for scientific insight

Winter to pre-monsoon

Tests whether winter/spring states provide predictive context before onset.

Pre-monsoon to onset

Onset to peak

Measures ability to forecast strengthening rainfall phases.

Peak to retreat

Tests model behavior during monsoon weakening and spatial redistribution.

Retreat to post-monsoon

Highlights post-monsoon and coastal/orographic anomaly difficulty.

Evaluates predictability during low-rainfall and transition months.
