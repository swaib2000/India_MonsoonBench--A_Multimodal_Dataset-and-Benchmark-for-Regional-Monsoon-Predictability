A multimodal benchmark for Indian rainfall anomaly forecasting.

India MonsoonBench is a spatially aligned, analysis-ready raster dataset for regional monsoon modeling. It combines rainfall anomaly labels with precipitation history, land surface temperature, NDVI, humidity, soil moisture, wind speed, and elevation across Indian hydroclimatic regimes.

Core Contribution

Not just files: a benchmark suite for monsoon ML.

The dataset is designed as a reproducible benchmark for monthly rainfall anomaly prediction. Our strongest formulation is autoregressive: the model observes the previous three months and predicts the rainfall anomaly class for the next month. Splits are temporally strict, using earlier years for training and 2024 for testing.

We intentionally describe the resource as a spatially aligned multimodal raster benchmark rather than claiming that every modality has identical native resolution. This is the scientifically safer framing: each source preserves its native information content while being provided on a common analysis grid for ML workflows.
