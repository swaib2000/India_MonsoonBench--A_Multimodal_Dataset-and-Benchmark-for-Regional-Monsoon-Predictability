Benchmark Suite

Strict temporal forecasting tasks with regional diagnostics.

The current benchmark suite includes annual next-year forecasting and monthly autoregressive regional forecasting. The monthly AR task is the strongest scientific formulation for the rebuttal.

Monthly AR Input

19 channels: elevation plus six dynamic modalities across three previous months.

Temporal Split

Train on 2021-2022, validate on 2023, and test on 2024 for regional monthly tasks.

Evaluation

Accuracy, weighted F1, ordinal distance, extreme recall, and transition-specific performance.

Non-Neural Baselines

Persistence and seasonal climatology baselines

Persistence predicts the previous month rainfall class for the same spatial patch. Seasonal climatology predicts the most frequent training label for each state and target month. These baselines are intentionally strong: they test whether deep models add value beyond seasonal and local-memory priors.

Model vs Baseline

Best neural model compared with best simple baseline

The comparison is deliberately conservative. Neural models improve over simple baselines in northwest and south regions, while seasonal climatology remains stronger in the current central and east-northeast runs. This strengthens the benchmark claim: the dataset exposes genuine seasonal structure and region-specific difficulty rather than trivial classification.

Ablation Insight

Multimodal predictors matter differently by region.

Modality ablations on Conv3D reveal that removing NDVI produces a large drop in the annual benchmark, indicating that vegetation response carries useful information about rainfall anomaly regimes. Wind removal also degrades performance, while LST and soil moisture have region-dependent effects. This supports the dataset's value as a multimodal diagnostic resource rather than only a rainfall-label benchmark.

Scientific Diagnostics

Transition-specific evaluation turns model errors into climate questions

Monsoon onset and peak

Northwest and south-regional analyses show different model strengths across pre-monsoon to monsoon-onset and monsoon-peak transitions, suggesting region-specific predictability.

Extreme recall

Scarcity and large-excess recall expose where models fail to detect rare but high-impact regimes. These diagnostics are more scientifically informative than accuracy alone.
