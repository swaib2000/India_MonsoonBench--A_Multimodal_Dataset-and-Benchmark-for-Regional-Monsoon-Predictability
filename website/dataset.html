Dataset Design

Spatially aligned raster stacks for Indian monsoon anomaly research.

The dataset provides ML-ready raster stacks and patch benchmarks built from GEE exports, IMD-derived rainfall normals, and state-boundary masking.

5 years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024
60 monthly dynamic bands per predictor
65 rainfall label bands including annual totals
15 x 15 default spatial patch size

Resolution and limitations

The benchmark is distributed on a common raster grid for modeling, but source variables have different native resolutions and physical meanings. Coarser variables such as humidity, soil moisture, and wind speed should be interpreted as aligned contextual fields rather than true high-resolution measurements. The temporal span is 2020-2024, making the resource most appropriate for benchmark and short-period anomaly modeling rather than long-term climatological trend attribution.
